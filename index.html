About Me

Hi, I’m Kai. I’m a recent Computer Science graduate from the University of Central Florida. During my studies, I worked on a team that developed a prototype passive aircraft detection system for U.S. SOUTHCOM and the U.S. Navy, which gave me experience collaborating on a real-world, mission-driven project.

I also participated in a summer cohort with the Defense Innovation Unit, where I was exposed to agile development, early-stage product development, and the business side of technology. Since graduating, I’ve continued building my technical foundation through personal projects—including software and game development—while actively learning new tools and technologies.
